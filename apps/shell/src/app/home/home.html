Portfolio sites show the résumé. This one shows the graph. A decade of platform work taught me this: when several products share a frontend, they belong in one Nx workspace — apps depend on libs, CI is nx affected, a CSS-only change does not pretend to be a backend.

Apps is a collection of my projects on this domain. Each is its own Angular app. They share chrome and tokens. They load as full pages, not a federated SPA. I built it in public to show how I actually ship enterprise frontends.
